Privacy Policy for Transcript Grabber

Chrome extension "Transcript Grabber for YouTube" · Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version

  • The extension works inside your browser. We have no server and we do not collect, see or store your data.
  • Your history, bookmarks, highlights, settings and keys stay on your computer.
  • Text is sent to Google, Anthropic or Notion only when you press a button that uses your own key or token for that service.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell data.

1. Who we are

2. What the extension does

It lets you read, search, translate, copy, download and summarize the transcript of a YouTube video, save highlights and bookmarks, and turn a video into notes or a one-page image.

4. Features that send data to others

  • AI with your key. If you add a Gemini or Claude key, the transcript or comments you selected, and your question, are sent to that provider to get an answer. That provider's own privacy policy applies. Google states that prompts on its free Gemini tier may be used to improve its products, so avoid confidential material when using a free key.
  • Notion. If you add a Notion token and a page, the content you choose to send is sent to Notion.
  • Translation. Translated captions are requested from YouTube, using YouTube's own caption translation.
  • On-device AI. If your Chrome supports built-in AI, it runs on your computer.
  • ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Obsidian buttons. These copy a prompt to your clipboard and open the site or app. The extension does not send anything to them. You decide whether to paste it.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not run a server and we do not receive your data.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, credit or lending decisions.
  • We do not use analytics, trackers or remote code.
  • No person at our end reads your data, because it never reaches us.

6. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Access to youtube.com: to read captions and show the transcript panel on video pages.
  • Access to api.anthropic.com, generativelanguage.googleapis.com and api.notion.com: only to make the requests you start with your own key or token.
  • Storage: to keep your history, bookmarks and settings on your computer.
  • Active tab and scripting: to read the transcript from the YouTube tab you are using when you open the extension.
  • Context menu: for the "Get transcript" right-click option.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Use Clear history in the Library tab to remove saved transcripts. Delete individual bookmarks and highlights in the panel.
  • Remove your keys and tokens by clearing the fields and saving, or by deleting the keys at the provider.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all data it stored.

8. Security

Keys and tokens are stored in your browser's extension storage on your own computer. Anyone with access to your browser profile could read them, so keep your computer and Chrome profile secure, and use keys you can delete or replace.

9. Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
